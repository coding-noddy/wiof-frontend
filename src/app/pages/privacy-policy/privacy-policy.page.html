Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

World Is One Family ("WIOF", "we", "us", or "our") operates this website and its related features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

We do not sell, rent, or trade email addresses or other personal information. Information is used only to operate, secure, improve, and communicate about the WIOF application and, where you choose to join it, the WIOF newsletter.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides us with identifiers such as your user ID, name, email address, and profile photo URL, subject to the information available from your Google account.
  • Profile information: the elements you choose (in the welcome after you first sign in, or in Settings), whether you have finished or skipped that welcome and hidden the first-steps checklist, and account activity such as registration date, login count, visit days, streaks, and saved-content counts.
  • Engagement information: saved content, blog reading completion (including the element of the story), video watching completion, poll votes, EQ assessment completion and scores, widget usage, Take Action progress, completions and streaks, and daily visits. These records are associated with your account so that features such as My Journey and personalised suggestions can work.
  • EQ assessment: if you are not signed in, your answers are scored in your browser and nothing is stored. If you are signed in, your answers are scored on our servers and up to two results a day are saved to your EQ history; further results that day are shown to you but not saved.
  • Poll votes: the option you choose. When you are signed in, your vote is linked to your account and its email address. When you are not signed in, you may optionally enter an email address; we use it only to prevent duplicate votes.
  • Feedback you send: the category and message you write, your name and email address if you choose to give them, the page you sent it from, your browser's user agent, and, if you are signed in, your user ID so that you can follow its status on My Feedback.
  • Location searches: place names you type into the air quality or rainfall tools are sent to the data providers that power them (see below). We do not access your device's location.
  • Newsletter information: name and email address when you voluntarily subscribe.
  • Technical information: browser, device, approximate usage information, pages or features used, dates and times, and network information made available by the browser or service providers.

How we use information

  • Provide authentication, profiles, saved content, and My Journey.
  • Record and display your engagement history and progress.
  • Check that activity records are genuine (for example, that a story was actually read through) before they count towards your progress, to keep progress fair and prevent misuse.
  • Suggest content and actions to you (see "Personalised suggestions" below).
  • Operate polls (including preventing duplicate votes), content, environmental tools, Take Action, and site features.
  • Review, respond to, and show you the status of feedback you send.
  • Send newsletter communications when you subscribe.
  • Understand usage, improve the website, and troubleshoot failures.
  • Protect the website, prevent misuse, and enforce our terms.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Personalised suggestions

When you are signed in, WIOF suggests stories, videos and small actions for you, for example in "Next for you", "Your first steps" and the summary on My Journey. These suggestions are made by simple, fixed rules from the elements you chose (or the ones you have explored), the stories you have read to the end, and the actions you have started or completed. Each suggestion shows why it was made. They are not used for advertising, are not shared with anyone, and are not used to make any decision that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Suggestions on a page itself, such as "Explore next" and "Today on WIOF", are based on the content you are looking at, not on information about you, and are the same for every visitor. You can change your elements in Settings at any time, and Delete my data in Settings clears the history that personalised suggestions use.

Legal bases and permissions

Depending on where you live, we process information to provide requested services, with your consent, for legitimate operational interests, and to comply with law. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already performed lawfully.

Cookies and local storage

  • Firebase Authentication may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain a sign-in session.
  • We use browser local storage to remember that you have acknowledged the privacy banner, seen the welcome message, or subscribed to the newsletter, that you hid "Today on WIOF" for the day, and certain application preferences. This stays on your device.
  • Firebase Analytics may collect usage and device information when enabled. You can control cookies and storage through your browser; disabling them may affect sign-in or application functionality.
  • Videos are embedded from YouTube, which may set its own cookies and collect information under Google's privacy policy when a video loads or plays.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate WIOF, including Google and Firebase for authentication, hosting, database, storage, and analytics, and YouTube for embedded videos. Environmental tools use third-party data services, including the World Air Quality Index project (air quality) and Open-Meteo (weather, rainfall, and place search). These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services.

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or legal process; to protect rights, safety, and security; to investigate fraud or abuse; or as part of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of the service. We do not disclose information for third-party advertising or sell user lists.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries different from your own. Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account operation, legal, security, and record-keeping needs. You may request deletion of your account and personal information. Some information may be retained where required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Your privacy choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, update, export, restrict, or delete your personal information, or object to a particular use. When signed in, you can permanently clear your saved content and engagement history yourself in Settings, using Delete my data; to delete your account itself, contact us. You may unsubscribe from newsletter messages using the available unsubscribe mechanism or by contacting us. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including Firebase authentication and database access rules. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

WIOF is not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us.

External websites

WIOF may link to third-party websites, videos, maps, or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date and, where required, provide additional notice for material changes.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, including deleting your account, use the Share feedback button at the bottom of any page and choose Something else. Please include an email address so we can reply.
